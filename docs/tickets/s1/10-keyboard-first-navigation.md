# 10: Keyboard-first navigation

**What to build:** Linear-style keys everywhere per DESIGN.md Interaction: `j/k` move the Cursor through rows and cards (`h/l` across kanban columns), `⏎` open, `a` approve, `d` add dependency, `s` status (a picker limited to the table's allowed edges), `esc` back, `⌘K` command palette (search anything, open it, act on the current Ticket), `⌘1–6` focus a tile. `c` (new ticket) is already built; `n` stays retired. Every tile header shows its keys; one tile is focused (border in `--gf-system`). Keys always do exactly what the equivalent drag or button does — never more.

**Blocked by:** 05 (Dependencies and the ready frontier)

**Status:** built 2026-09-04

**Decisions (grilled 2026-09-03):**

- *Vocabulary.* CONTEXT.md gains **Focused tile**, **Cursor** (with *current Ticket*: the open Ticket on a Ticket view, otherwise the Ticket under the Cursor) and **Selection** (03b's set). This ticket moves a Cursor; it never selects. Its earlier `n` was drift — 03 retired it for `c`; DESIGN.md and story 43 agree.
- *Cursor.* None on load; the first `j` lands on the first card of the first non-empty column. `j/k` within a column, `h/l` to the same row of the neighbouring non-empty column (clamped to its last card), arrows as aliases; no `gg`/`G`/`0`/`$`. Keyed by ticket key: survives the 5 s poll, dropped when the card leaves the board, follows a card that a transition moves. Remembered so that `⏎` then `esc` restores it to the card you opened, if it is still on the board. The mark is Shapes' row selection — a 2px `--gf-system` left inset — on cards and rows alike; the full system border stays the drag's.
- *Tile focus.* `⌘1–6` in reading order per page (Ticket: about, design, dependencies, state, history; Project: tickets, graph, design, about, history; App: projects, tickets, about, history). Chrome hands `⌘1–6` to a page that `preventDefault`s them (verified 2026-09-03 with a keydown test page), so `⌘` ships as DESIGN.md says. Focus follows click; resets to tile 1 on navigation. `j/k/⏎` and `e` act inside the focused tile; `e` keeps its rule (the focused tile's editor, else the first on the page). Bare `1 2 3` stay the workspace keys.
- *Where `a s d` act.* On the current Ticket: the board (cursor card) and the Ticket view only. App/Project `tickets` and `projects` tiles, the all-apps/all-projects lists and dependency rows get `j/k/⏎` (open) and nothing else — they have no transition buttons, and keys never do more than the GUI. Trash rows keep `r`; there is nothing to open.
- *Refusals.* `a` with no `approve` arrow from the current status says the structural refusal locally from the shared table (as the drop does); guard refusals come from the API. Every key's refusal lands in the slot its button uses: under the cursor's column on the board; on the Ticket view the state tile's line for a transition, the about tile's line for a refused move.
- *`s` and ⌘K are one component.* `s` opens the palette pre-scoped to the current Ticket's allowed transitions, `cancel` last. The blocked-`start` confirm shows in its usual slot after the palette closes, never inside it.
- *`d`.* Ticket view: focus the dependencies tile and open the `blocked by` picker. Board: navigate to the cursor ticket with that picker already open (`location.state`) — go where the button is and press it.
- *⌘K contents.* Groups in fixed order: **tickets** by key or title (exact key `GF-12` or bare `12` first, open before `done`/`cancelled`); **actions** on the current Ticket, only when there is one — the transitions, `trash`, `flag simple`/`unflag simple`, `blocked by…` (what `d` does), `move to project…` / `move to app…` as a nested pick of live targets (ADR-0007 applied as the Ticket view's selects apply it); **go to** — board, projects, apps, trash, settings, every live app and project by name (archived hidden, CONTEXT.md); **create** — `new ticket` (the `c` form in scope), `new app`, `new project` (the `+` forms; the `Plus` comment promised this here); **clear filters** on the board (Components' filter-bar note promised this here). Empty query shows everything but tickets; case-insensitive substring; at most 8 rows. Searches every live ticket regardless of the board's Filter. The action target is "the current ticket(s)", so 03b's Selection plugs in later without reshaping the palette.
- *Palette shape.* A floating panel at the top centre of the desk on `--gf-raised` in a 1px `--gf-rule`, the picker popover's clothes: `--gf-system` input, rows in the Ticket picker shape with a mono caps group label, `↑↓ ⏎ esc`. No backdrop, no dimming; click outside closes. The dmenu of this window manager, not a dialog. Inside an editor `⌘K` remains the link command — a typing target already silences global keys.
- *`esc`.* Closes the topmost open thing first (palette, picker, view menu, create row; blurs an editor or the filter input — all existing). With nothing open: on the board it clears the cursor; elsewhere it goes back through in-app history (`history.state.idx > 0`), else to the board.
- *Tile headers.* Every tile shows its `⌘n`. The focused tile adds `j k ⏎` when its body is rows or cards (history is not navigable). The board tile and the state tile show `a s d`. `⌘K` sits in the bar at the right, beside `trash` and `settings`. Unfocused tiles show only their number and their own keys.
- *Not in 10.* Multi-select and bulk actions (03b); touch buttons for keyboard-only actions (11); `copy key`; vim range keys; a `?` key sheet. No ADR — nothing here is hard to reverse.

**Close-out deviations (2026-09-04).** `/impeccable critique` on the board and the Ticket view, then Matt's two-axis review, moved five decisions above. Drift is allowed; unexplained drift is not.

- *"Every tile shows its `⌘n`."* A tile carries its number only where the desk holds **more than one** tile — the board, `/apps`, `/projects` and `/settings` are one-tile desks, and a number with nothing to switch to is not information. Both critiques counted the board's ten-token header as the screen's worst cognitive load.
- *"The board tile and the state tile show `a s d`."* Both now advertise `a approve` only where the transition table has that arrow out of the current status, exactly as the state tile already draws its buttons. A hint that cannot work is worse than no hint (DESIGN.md Voice), and `approve` is not an arrow out of `ready`.
- *"at most 8 rows"* still holds, but with an empty query the eight are shared out a row at a time across the groups present. The `actions` group alone is nine or ten rows on a Ticket, so the fixed group order was burying `go to`, `create` and `clear filters` entirely — which contradicted this ticket's own "empty query shows everything but tickets".
- *"`cancel` last"* still holds; the rows before it now lead with the moves that carry the Ticket forward, because `⏎` on an untyped query takes the first row and the table's own order opens with `unapprove` out of `ready`. The state tile's buttons keep table order — they *are* the table; a palette is ranked by what you probably meant.
- *"a mono caps group label"* — the `actions` group label and the `s` placeholder also name the current Ticket (`actions · GF-3`). The panel covers the card it is about, and `trash` and `cancel` are one `⏎` from a subject otherwise held only in working memory.

Two more things changed outside this ticket's scope, and are recorded where they belong: `--gf-mute` was lifted to `#A3AFA6` so it clears 4.5:1 on `--gf-raised` (this ticket put a panel of 10.5px hints on that surface; DESIGN.md Colors now measures the text-safe rule against the raised step), and three findings this ticket does not own went to `docs/tickets/later/`.

Read alongside CONTEXT.md, *"None on load"* means before the first press, not across a reload: CONTEXT.md's **Cursor** says a refresh does not lose it, so the memory is `sessionStorage`. Seeding on any of `j/k/h/l` rather than `j` alone is the same reading — a key that appears dead is worse than one that starts you at the top.
